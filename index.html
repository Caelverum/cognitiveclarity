Why Cognitive Clarity Matters

Cognitive warfare is the contest for cognitive superiority. It aims to shape perception, exhaust attention, and degrade decision-making — often below the threshold of open conflict.

🧠 The Mind is the Target

Unlike cyber or kinetic attacks, cognitive warfare goes after the human decision-making process itself — biases, attention, trust, and sense-making.

⚡ It Operates Continuously

It does not wait for declared war. Information overload, polarisation, narrative flooding and algorithmic amplification run every day.

🛡️ Clarity is Resilience

Protecting your attention, pruning low-quality inputs, and strengthening critical thinking are the practical defences available to every individual.
